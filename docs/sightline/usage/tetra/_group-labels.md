A group must have a name. The **New group** drawer marks **Label** as required and
does not create the group until it holds text.

## Other entities

| Entity | Label |
|---|---|
| Group | Required |
| Zone, device, network, information, system | Required |
| Channel, flow | Optional |

A channel or flow can exist with only its two endpoints, and Tetra also creates
unlabelled channels for device-to-network adjacency. A relationship label is
therefore optional. The relationship's **Label display** setting chooses what the
diagram writes on the line.
